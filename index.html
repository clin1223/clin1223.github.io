Chuang Lin

I am currently a Researcher at ByteDance Seed. I received my Ph.D. degree from Monash University in 2024, where I was supervised by Prof. Jianfei Cai. Previously, I obtained my M.Eng. degree from Harbin Institute of Technology and B.Eng. degree from Jilin University.

I'm broadly interested in computer vision and deep learning. My current research focuses on foundation models for visual generation and interactive world modeling.
